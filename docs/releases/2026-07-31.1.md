# 2026-07-31.1

## Notifications

Astro AI now tells you when something needs your attention. A bell in the header opens an in-app inbox, and you can opt into email for the same events. Notifications cover build failures, billing (failed payments, action required, spend thresholds, recovery, suspension), team membership changes, account ownership transfers, and security key changes. A settings page lets you choose, per notification, which channels it uses.

- **Take you to the problem.** A notification's "view" link now opens the exact resource it's about in the right account, not a generic page.
- **Welcome on sign-up.** Creating a new account now sends a welcome notification.

## Alerts for running agents

Deployed agents are now watched continuously, and you're alerted when one starts failing or degrading — crash loops, out-of-memory kills, restart storms, pods that can't be scheduled, memory or compute pressure, and wasteful over-provisioning. A new per-workload **Alerts** tab on the deployment page shows each condition's live state.

- **Three tiers.** Health problems arrive as critical or warning; cost/waste "over-provisioned" findings are a separate lower-priority tier with its own toggle, so they never crowd out real outages.
- **Plain language, with specifics.** Alerts greet you by name and spell out what happened and what to do — observed usage, a right-sizing suggestion, restart counts — instead of raw Kubernetes reasons.
- **No spam.** A flapping agent that keeps recovering and re-breaking sends at most one alert per issue per day.

## AI-assisted review

The trace review queue now shows AI judgment predictions: a suggested verdict, confidence, rationale, and a per-criterion assessment. Agree with the judge in one click (it preselects the accepted and rejected criteria) or pick your own verdict. A **Run AI Judge** button kicks off judging in the background across recent traces, links estimated usage to billing, and is disabled while judging is in flight or when nothing is left to judge.

## Insights: Data Sources and privacy controls

- **Exclude people from content capture.** An ingestion key can now exclude specific people so their prompts, responses, and tool calls are dropped at ingest while their usage metadata (tokens, cost, latency) is still counted. Editable anytime without revoking the key.
- **Data Sources page.** The ingest-key settings page is now **Data Sources**, and Insights gains an always-visible External sources section with an "Add a source" entry.
- **Tell people from bots.** The People table now marks automated traffic with an "Agent" or "Bot" badge next to non-human identities.
- Insights tables (Agents, People, Models) now share the same column order, so columns stay put when you switch views.

## Your resources across every account

Blueprints, Knowledge Stores, and Agents now show what you own across every account you belong to, filtered by a lightweight account multi-select instead of switching your whole session. One slow or failing account no longer hides the others, and filter selections are shareable by link.

- **See who deployed.** Deployment history now shows which team member kicked off each deployment — active and past revisions — with avatar and name.
- **Filters that stick.** List and analytics filters survive navigation, so returning to a page restores your working view.
- **Find any agent fast.** The quick agent selector has a pinned search box that matches agent and account names across all your accounts.
- Switching the account filter reveals results with a subtle fade (respecting reduced-motion), and the agent search box no longer shows a duplicate browser clear button.

## Knowledge stores

- **Connect Supabase.** Supabase is a new knowledge-store type: sign in with Supabase, pick a project, enter only the database password, and the rest of the connection is filled in for you.
- **Edit a connection.** An external store's connection details can now be updated in place from its Danger Zone; the new credentials are health-checked before they're saved, so a bad edit can't leave the store unusable.
- Supabase stores now connect over the session pooler, so they're reachable and stop failing their health check with "network is unreachable".
- Binding an external knowledge store to an agent no longer leaves the deployment stuck "deploying" waiting on a workload that never appears.

## Models

- **Pick the model at deploy time.** The Astro AI gateway is now a first-class model provider: choose from a dropdown of allowed models when you deploy, instead of a fixed on/off flag.
- Self-hosted models must now be declared as a custom container; Ollama is no longer a built-in provider.

## Deploy reliability

- **No more forever-deploying.** A deploy that stalls now fails on its own after a deadline so you can retry, and a deploy that references a missing or malformed secret/config fails in seconds with the specific reason instead of hanging.
- **Stateful agents recover.** A stateful agent wedged on a bad or missing image after redeploy now unsticks itself automatically.
- **Redeploy a paused agent.** Paused agents can be redeployed (and their config saved) directly, with a note that doing so will reactivate the agent; resuming one now updates the UI immediately instead of showing contradictory states.
- **Jump to the failure.** When an agent fails to start, the failing workload's diagnostics panel now opens automatically.
- Deploy-form autofill no longer overwrites your edits or restores values you cleared, and saved secrets stay hidden and are never sent back to the browser.

## Chat

- **Structured questions inline.** An agent can now pause a turn to ask a structured question — approve a tool call, fill a short form, pick from a list — rendered in the composer instead of asking you to type free text.
- **Turns end cleanly.** A crashed or hung agent now reliably ends the turn with a clear error, and a reply that's still streaming no longer shows a false "timed out" message.
- **Clearer file errors.** The Files panel and attachment chips now show actionable errors (bad request, missing file, too large, storage full, auth, temporary outage), per file in a batch.
- Opening chat for a stopped, paused, or deploying agent no longer errors in the background; history shows empty and upload controls stay hidden until the agent is reachable.

## Billing

Payment problems now move an account through the right states: a failed payment enters past-due/dunning, an uncollectible write-off suspends the account, and paying or voiding an invoice — or an automatically updated card — clears the block.

- The editable public-profile contact email field is removed from profile settings; your login/identity email is unaffected.

## Security

Three cross-tenant vulnerabilities were fixed: per-agent GitHub routes now enforce account membership; the knowledge-store connect health check can no longer be aimed at internal infrastructure; and the messaging proxy no longer allows path traversal into the Kubernetes API. See the migration note about private-only knowledge stores.

## Admin console (Queen)

- A cross-deployment **Alerts** page for visibility into and management of active alerts across every deployment, with a last-notified time column.
- The deployment-detail page is reworked around a lifecycle state graph with per-container cards, and sidecars (e.g. messaging) are now visible.

## Polish

- Connectors settings redesigned: available services and connected accounts grouped in a single surface with icon tiles and scope-aware summaries for GitHub orgs and Slack workspaces.
- Fixed backgrounds and surfaces that looked off after recent theme changes (notably blueprint detail pages); draft blueprint cards get a subtle distinct tint.
- Knowledge container tiles in the pod graph now show live disk usage with a fill bar that turns amber past 80% and red past 95%.
- The Database monitor tab now appears only for agents that actually have a knowledge base; the Requests & Latency chart keeps its x-axis at smaller widths.
- Org admins (not just the owner) can now create and revoke ingest keys.
- The not-found page for a deleted or unknown agent now reads "Agent not found", and opening a deleted deployment shows a clear not-found panel instead of crashing.
