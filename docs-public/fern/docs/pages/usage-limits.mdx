title: Usage limits
subtitle: Warn yourself before an account spends too much, and stop it before it spends more

Every account can cap what it spends. Open **Manage limits** on the Pay-as-you-go card, on the billing page of your account settings.

There are two numbers:

- **Alert threshold** sends a notification and changes nothing else.
- **Spend limit** stops every agent in the account.

Leave a field empty for no limit. The alert threshold has to be below the spend limit, because the limit stops the account first.

## When a limit stops your agents

Every agent in the account stops. Deploying, restarting, and waking an agent are refused until the account starts again.

Three things start it again:

- Raise the limit above what the period has already used.
- Remove the limit.
- Wait for the next billing period, which resets what the period has counted.

Raising a limit takes effect immediately. Raising it to a number the period has already passed leaves the account stopped, because the account is still over the new limit.

## When your card expires

A card that has passed its expiry month counts as no card on file. Astro AI checks
the card it has saved once a day, marks it **Expired** on the billing page, and
stops treating the account as pay-as-you-go.

What happens next depends on the signup credit:

- Credit left: nothing stops. The account keeps running on the credit.
- Credit spent: every agent in the account stops, the same way it would if you had
  never added a card.

Save a new card on the billing page to start the account again. If your bank
reissues the card with a new expiry date, Astro AI picks that up on its own and
nothing stops.

## Signup credit

A new personal account gets a one-time credit to spend before it needs a card. Astro AI shows the amount once, right after you create the account. To see how much of the period's total you've spent, open [Usage](/usage).
