# Typography

Composite font-size tokens that apply size, line-height, letter-spacing, and (for headings) font-weight in one Tailwind class. Pair with standard utilities for family and text-transform.

## Full Scale

<Canvas of={TypographyStories.FullScale} />

## Tokens

| Token class | Size | Line-height | Letter-spacing | Typical pairing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `text-display` | 46px | 1.1 | -0.025em | semibold (built-in) |
| `text-heading-1` | 28px | 1 | -0.02em | semibold (built-in) |
| `text-heading-2` | 18px | 1 | -0.01em | semibold (built-in) |
| `text-heading-3` | 16px | 1 | -0.01em | semibold (built-in) |
| `text-heading-4` | 14px | 1 | — | semibold (built-in) |
| `text-body` | 14px | 1.65 | — | — |
| `text-body-sm` | 12px | 1.55 | — | — |
| `text-label` | 11px | 1 | 0.18em | `font-mono uppercase` |
| `text-mono-md` | 14px | 1 | 0.02em | `font-mono` |
| `text-mono-sm` | 12px | 1 | 0.07em | `font-mono` |

## Usage

```tsx
<h1 className="text-heading-1">Page Title</h1>
<p className="text-body">Description text goes here.</p>
<span className="text-label font-mono uppercase">Active</span>
```
